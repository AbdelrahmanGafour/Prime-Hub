Details

This wax is suitable for blocking undercuts, cavities or errors on plaster models for both model casting and crown as well as for bridge techniques.

Advantages

  • No dimensional changes during processes involving heat.
  • Ideal for duplication with duplicating gels or agar-agar.
  • Very high adhesive qualities even on a damp model.
