Details

The Easyclean MD ultrasonic cleaning unit is used for powerful cleaning or pre-cleaning* of medical devices such as e.g. abutments and dentures as well as instrumentation (*does not replace reprocessing in the automated washer disinfector).

Advantages

  • As a Class I medical device, Easyclean MD meets the requirements of the Medical Devices Regulation (EU) 2017/745.
  • With five pre-configured ultrasonic modes, the right program can be activated for any situation.
  • Intuitive operation facilitates effortless creation of custom programs.
