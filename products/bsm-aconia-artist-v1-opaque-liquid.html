Details

Aconia Artist V1 is brushed in a single layer inside the crown to cover discolored stumps or metal abutments, keeping crown thickness unchanged for both high-translucency and ultra-thin zirconia.

Advantages

  • Keeps crown thickness unchanged
  • Works for high-translucency and ultra-thin zirconia
