Who We Are

Built on Trust, Driven by Quality

Prime Hub Trading was founded in Cairo in 2010 with a simple goal: give dental laboratories and clinics across Egypt reliable access to the materials, equipment and technical know-how they need to deliver excellent restorative work. What began as a small supplier of core laboratory materials has grown into a full-service partner spanning digital CAD/CAM equipment, authorized technical support and hands-on training.

Today we represent 8 international manufacturing brands and support more than 1,000 laboratories and clinics nationwide, but our approach hasn't changed - understand what our customers need, source the right products, and stand behind them with real service.
